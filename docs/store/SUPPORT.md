## Common questions

**Why does it say DEMO on the prices?**
You're in demo mode, against a fictional market. Every store and price is invented, so
Juva labels them rather than letting them look real. Nothing in the app can show demo data
as live.

**Why couldn't Juva price something?**
Its price coverage is narrow and honest. Juva reads community-contributed price data, so a
product nobody near you has contributed a price for has no price Juva can use. It reports
that item as unpriced instead of guessing — an invented price would corrupt the whole
basket total.

**Why is my saving different from the estimate?**
The estimate was the plan; the verified figure is your receipt. Shelf prices change, and
promotions expire. Juva keeps the two separate and shows both, and only receipt-confirmed
trips count toward your verified savings total.

**Juva says my saving is $0.00. Is it broken?**
Probably not. If your receipt total came out at or above the cheapest single-store basket
Juva found before the trip, you didn't save against that baseline — so Juva reports zero
rather than inventing a saving. The difference figure will show what actually happened.

**Where are my receipt photos?**
On your device only, for as long as you chose in Juva Space → Shopping preferences →
Receipt images. You can set that to "Not at all", in which case an image is deleted as soon
as it has been read. "Delete all receipt images now" removes every one immediately and
keeps the figures already read from them.

**Does a copy of my receipt get uploaded?**
Only when you tap to have one read, and only to Juva's own extraction API. It is not
written to disk there, not logged, and not retained after the response. Crop the footer
first if you'd rather the card digits never leave — that happens on your device.

**How do I cancel Juva Plus?**
Through your app store, not through Juva — an app can't cancel a store subscription. Juva
Space → Subscription links you straight to the right page.

**I paid on another device.**
Juva Space → Subscription → Restore purchases.

**How do I delete everything?**
Juva Space → Shopping preferences → Data → "Delete all local Juva data". There is no
account and no server copy, so that is genuinely all of it.
