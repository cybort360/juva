# Terms of use

_Last updated: 12 August 2026._

## What Juva is

Juva is a planning tool. It compares grocery prices it can observe and suggests where to
shop. It is not a retailer, does not sell groceries, and is not affiliated with, endorsed
by or acting for any retailer named or shown in the app.

## What Juva does not promise

**Prices may be wrong or out of date.** Juva shows prices from community-contributed data
and from prices you enter yourself. It labels how fresh each price is and where it came
from. It does not guarantee that a price shown is the price you will be charged, and the
shelf price at the till always governs.

**Savings are estimates until a receipt says otherwise.** A figure shown before a trip is a
calculation against the prices Juva could see at that moment. Juva separates estimated from
verified savings and only counts receipt-confirmed trips toward a verified total. Neither
figure is a promise of a future saving.

**Coverage is limited.** Juva can only reason about stores and prices available to it. Its
plans are not a survey of every store or price in your area.

**You decide where to shop.** Juva's recommendation is advice. Availability, quality and
substitution are matters for you and the retailer.

## Subscription

Juva Plus is an auto-renewing subscription sold through your app store and managed by
RevenueCat. Price and term are shown before purchase. Renewal, cancellation and refunds are
governed by your app store's terms, and cancellation is done there — Juva links you to it
but cannot do it for you. Free features remain free: the cheapest complete single-store
plan, Shop Mode, receipt verification and your verified savings.

## Your content

Your lists, trips, receipt figures and receipt images are yours and are stored on your
device. Receipt images are sent for text extraction only when you ask for one to be read.
You can delete any of it, and all of it, from within the app.

## Acceptable use

Don't use Juva to scrape or redistribute retailer data, and don't rely on it where being
wrong would matter more than the price of groceries.

## Liability

Juva is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses arising
from a price being wrong, a store being out of stock, a plan being suboptimal, or a saving
not materialising. Nothing here limits rights you have that cannot be limited.
