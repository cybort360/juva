# Store listing copy

Every claim below is one the app can back. There are no user counts, no savings averages
and no retailer names Juva does not actually read — because none of those are things we can
substantiate today.

## App name

`Juva` — subtitle: `Grocery plans that add up`

## Short description (Play, 80 char max)

> Plan a cheaper grocery shop nearby, then check the receipt actually matched.

(76 characters.)

## Full description

> Juva plans a grocery shop the way you'd do it if you had time to compare everything.
>
> Tell it what you need — "milk, eggs, rice, chicken, bread" or a pasted list — and Juva
> prices the basket across nearby stores. It shows the cheapest single store you could
> shop, and where it's worth splitting the trip across two, including what that split
> actually costs you in distance and minutes.
>
> Then it walks you through the shop, and afterwards it reads your receipt and reconciles
> it line by line against the plan. Estimated savings and verified savings are kept
> separate, always. A saving isn't real until a receipt says so.
>
> HOW IT DECIDES
> • Every price, total and saving is arithmetic on observed prices — never an estimate,
> never generated by an AI
> • Prices carry where they came from and how fresh they are; nothing stale is shown as
> current
> • Unpriced items are reported as unpriced, not quietly dropped from the total
> • The trade-off is yours: change maximum stores, radius, transport or brand flexibility
> and Juva genuinely re-plans
>
> SHOP MODE
> • A store-by-store checklist that works offline once the trip starts
> • Swipe to collect or skip; correct a shelf price if it's changed
> • Hand off to Maps for the specific store the plan was priced at
>
> RECEIPT VERIFICATION
> • Photograph a receipt — several pages for a long one — or just type the total
> • Crop out the footer before anything is sent; card digits never need to leave
> • Juva matches receipt lines to your plan, asks you about anything ambiguous rather
> than guessing, and shows expected vs actual vs difference
>
> JUVA PLUS
> • Multi-store optimization and the Worth-the-Trip controls
> • Unlimited baskets, searches and full verified-savings history
> • The cheapest single-store plan, Shop Mode and receipt verification are always free
>
> PRIVACY
> • No account, no sign-in
> • Your lists, trips and receipts are stored on your device
> • Receipt images are yours: keep them for as long as you choose, or not at all
>
> Juva's price coverage is deliberately narrow and honest rather than broad and invented.
> It reads community-contributed price data and open map data. It is not a national price
> database, and it will tell you when it doesn't know something.

## Screenshot captions

Six screens, in the order a shopper meets them. Captions state what is shown, nothing more.

1. **"Say what you need."** — the composer with the example basket.
2. **"Priced across nearby stores."** — the plan with total and saving.
3. **"See what a second stop is worth."** — Worth the Trip, with distance and minutes.
4. **"Every figure explains itself."** — the deterministic score breakdown.
5. **"Shop it offline."** — Shop Mode checklist mid-trip.
6. **"Then check it really added up."** — receipt reconciliation, expected vs actual.

All screenshots are taken in demo mode. The prices are fictional and the DEMO badge is
visible, which is deliberate: a store screenshot showing invented prices as if they were
live would be the same lie inside the app.
