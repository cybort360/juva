# Juva demo video script — 120 seconds

Shot in **demo mode** on a booted simulator or a device build. Every price on screen is
fictional and carries a DEMO badge; that badge stays visible in the recording, because
cropping it out to look more impressive would be the exact dishonesty the app is built
against.

**Before recording:** clear app data so onboarding is fresh, set Reduce Motion **off** so
the money reveal plays, and pre-write the basket text so nothing is typed on camera.

---

## 0:00–0:10 — The ask

**On screen:** composer. Tap the example chip "Weekly groceries for two under $80", then
the arrow. Basket resolves to interpreted items with quantities.

> "Weekly groceries for two, under eighty dollars. That's the whole input."

**Note:** let the interpreted quantities land on screen — "1 gallon", "12 large". The point
is that it parsed, not that it guessed.

---

## 0:10–0:20 — The search

**On screen:** tap "Find the best basket". Search stages advance.

> "Juva prices that basket across the stores it can actually see near you — store by
> store, not one average price for a chain."

**Note:** do not speed this up. The stages are named and honest; a sped-up blur looks like
a loading spinner pretending to work.

---

## 0:20–0:30 — The reveal

**On screen:** the plan hero. Total, then the SAVE figure counting up.

> "Twenty-four twenty-four across two stores. Six-twenty saved — and that number is
> subtraction, not a guess. No model touches it."

**Note:** hold on the DEMO badge for a beat. Say the word "demo" if the voiceover has room.

---

## 0:30–0:50 — Three ways to shop

**On screen:** scroll to "Other ways to shop". Tap between Juva Pick, One Stop, and the
cheapest. Then the "Why this plan" breakdown.

> "Juva's pick, the absolute cheapest, and everything-in-one-trip. And it shows its
> arithmetic: basket, travel, time, extra stops. Only the basket is money you pay — the
> rest is how it ranks the options, and it says so."

---

## 0:50–1:10 — Worth the Trip

**On screen:** the Worth the Trip controls. Change Maximum stores from 2 to 1. The plan
re-computes to a different total and a different explanation.

> "This is the part that isn't a filter. Drop to one store and it re-plans — new total,
> new reasoning. Here one store is thirty forty-four, so the saving genuinely goes to
> zero, and Juva says zero rather than finding something flattering to show."

**Note:** this beat is the strongest thing in the app. Let the $0.00 land.

---

## 1:10–1:30 — Shop Mode

**On screen:** "Shop this plan". The checklist. Tap one row to collect. Swipe one row right
to collect, one left to skip. Show the running subtotal.

> "Then it walks the shop with you. Swipe to collect, swipe the other way to skip, correct
> a shelf price if it's changed. This works with the phone in airplane mode — once the trip
> starts, nothing needs the network."

---

## 1:30–1:45 — Receipt verification

**On screen:** VERIFY. Type a total. Add. Verify my trip. Reconciliation appears.

> "Afterwards, the receipt. Photograph it or type the total. Juva matches it line by line
> against the plan — and where it isn't sure which line is which, it asks instead of
> guessing."

---

## 1:45–1:55 — Verified savings

**On screen:** the result screen. Expected, actual, difference, estimated, verified.

> "Expected, actual, difference. Estimated savings and verified savings, side by side and
> never added together. Only a trip whose receipt was actually read counts."

---

## 1:55–2:00 — Close

**On screen:** the home screen with the verified savings total.

> "Juva. Groceries that add up."

**What must NOT be said here.** The brief asks for real traction, revenue and growth
metrics in this slot. **There are none, so the slot is a product close instead.** Juva has
no users, no revenue and no growth to report; a number invented for this beat would
undermine every honest claim in the ninety seconds before it. If real metrics exist by
recording day, state them plainly with their source and date. If not, close on the product.
