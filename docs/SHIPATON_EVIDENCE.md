# Shipaton Evidence Ledger

Capture evidence as the product progresses. Do not recreate metrics at submission time.

## Product

- first public store release date and version;
- target stores/platforms;
- screenshot of the natural-language composer;
- video of live-market orchestration;
- plan reveal showing the defined single-store baseline;
- Shop Mode recording;
- receipt-verification recording.

## RevenueCat

- Test Store purchase success;
- cancellation and failure paths;
- `juva_plus` active entitlement;
- monthly and annual packages only;
- first real transaction once live.

## Growth

For each experiment record date, channel, spend, impressions, clicks, installs, activated users, optimized baskets, verified trips, paywall views, purchases, revenue and learning.

## Truthfulness

Controlled-market screenshots must be labeled demo. Never submit demo-market savings as user savings, revenue or traction.
